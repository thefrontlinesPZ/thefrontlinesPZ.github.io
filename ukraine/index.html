Zero Line

Russian dugouts behind the tree line are pinning your company in its trench. Cross 1,500 metres of cratered no-man's land, past dragon's teeth and razor wire, clear the forward trench and put a demo charge on every dugout. You have one life. Wounds bleed until you bandage them. FPV drones hunt anyone in the open, so shoot them down or keep moving. Search the dead for ammunition. Two Leopard 2s are dug in behind you, and you can drive them.
